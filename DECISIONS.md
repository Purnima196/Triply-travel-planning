#Decisions

1. Why this ingestion strategy over the obvious alternative you rejected?

I chose a structured local-data approach because existing travel apps mainly focus on famous cities and popular tourist spots. They often miss lesser-known local places that are beautiful, affordable, and offer a more authentic experience With Triply, I wanted to include these hidden destinations along with affordable stays, restaurants, activities, and transport options. I also wanted the recommendations to work with lower budgets, helping users adjust their trip according to what they can actually afford.

2. One trade-off you made under the time limit, and what you’d do with a real week.

Due to the limited time, I had to prioritize the core functionality over extensive UI refinement and data collection. With a full week, I would further improve the UI, collect better destination photos, compare real prices for stays and restaurants, and add more local activities such as amusement parks. I would also collect more detailed data to make Triply more user-friendly and help users plan a complete trip within the lowest possible budget.

3. Where did you use AI tools, and what did you personally verify or change afterward?

I used AI tools mainly during the idea selection, development of some parts, coding of certain features, and UI refinement stages. AI helped me explore different product ideas, connect different parts of the code, solve technical issues, and learn how to improve pages and features and also used AI to help create the initial dataset. However, I personally verified and expanded the data by researching places through Google and adding lesser-known destinations, including some places I had personally visited. I also made my own changes to the UI, icons, content, and overall product experience.
